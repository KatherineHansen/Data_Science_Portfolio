Data Management

Super Mario 64 speedruns

Python · Speedrun.com API · Pandas · Matplotlib

An exploration of Super Mario 64 speedrun data pulled live from the Speedrun.com API. After paging through verified runs for the 16, 70, and 120 Star categories, I summarized submissions by year and found that run volume has declined since 2022 while the 16 Star category remains the most popular. I then pulled the top 100 16 Star world records, plotted completion times over time with the top three record holders annotated, and derived year-over-year improvements and the per-rank time gaps between records. The distribution of top-100 times shows how tightly clustered the leaderboard has become, quantifying just how competitive the top of the category is.

World Bank indicators & COVID-19 (data cleaning)

Python · Pandas · Excel · CSV output for Tableau

Starting from three datasets — COVID-19 cases, country populations, and World Bank indicators — I cleaned and organized them into a single combined dataset, then manipulated columns to derive COVID-19 rates per country and normalize formatting. Sell Short trades file cleanup

Python · Regular expressions · Text parsing

Steps included: removing unwanted lines (blanks, page numbers, repeated headers) by matching regex patterns; grouping records that spanned 2–4 lines into single rows; splitting fields into columns via regex; correcting data errors; and reordering columns to match the header.
