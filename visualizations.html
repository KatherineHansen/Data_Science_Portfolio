Fuel purchase & contract dashboard

Power BI · MS Access · Python (Selenium) · Web scraping

A Power BI dashboard tracking diesel purchases across distribution centers, enabling recommendations for fuel contracts using historical data and cost-efficiency analysis.

I managed an MS Access database by consolidating daily invoices from various sources, integrating EIA website data, and contracting information. I also connected to the AAA website through Power BI for state data, and web-scraped with Python (Selenium) to tie in local fuel prices reported by GasBuddy.

Visualizations

Minnesota early & absentee voting

Tableau · Excel · View on Tableau Public →

An exploration of the rising popularity of early and absentee voting in Minnesota presidential elections, with particular focus on 2020. The COVID-19 pandemic discouraged in-person voting and greatly encouraged absentee ballots. The visualizations cover two decades of elections, a county-level breakdown for 2016 and 2020, and an infographic of early-voting demographics.

World Bank indicators & COVID-19

Tableau · Python · Excel · View on Tableau Public →

An investigation into potential relationships between World Bank Development Indicators and COVID-19 case rates by country. I pulled data from multiple sources with most data management carried out in Python, then built Tableau graphs pairing each indicator (life expectancy, tobacco use, etc.) with country case rates — plus an interactive version where users can manipulate dates of interest and more.
