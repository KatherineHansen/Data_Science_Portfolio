About

I'm a data analytics developer at Colliers Engineering & Design, where I work on the Microsoft Fabric platform and build Power BI reports and dashboards for teams across the company. Most of my day is spent connecting data from different systems (SQL Server, SharePoint, Azure, files), cleaning it up, and making it usable for people who need it for their jobs.

Before this I was a data analyst at Russ Davis Wholesale, where I rebuilt the weekly reporting as SQL-driven automation and cut it from about 15 hours down to 3. I also built the sales and fuel dashboards there, which are the ones on this site.

I'm working on an M.S. in Analytics at Georgia Tech right now, which is where most of my experience with regression, clustering, and simulation comes from; it's less about making a dashboard look right and more about figuring out what the data is actually saying. Sometimes that means the answer isn't what was expected. The music clustering project is an example: the clusters didn't match the genre labels, and that ended up being the more interesting result.

I'm interested in roles that combine the reporting side with the analysis side, since I've spent the last few years doing both. If you want to get in touch, email is the easiest way to reach me.
