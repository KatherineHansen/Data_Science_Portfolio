Survey report automation (FCD)

Excel · Word · VBA

Freedom from Chemical Dependency Prevention Works (FCD) administers the Students Attitudes and Behavior Survey (SABS) to measure student attitudes and behaviors regarding alcohol and other drugs, as well as their perceptions of schoolmates' attitudes and behaviors. Results are communicated in a 100-page report. The goal of this project was to automate that report — using Microsoft Visual Basic for Applications (VBA), I determined ways to automate in-text inputs, tables, and charts from Excel to Word. While the full report was not completed, the finished portion runs much faster than the previous manual process.
